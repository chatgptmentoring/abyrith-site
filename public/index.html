The world of Athelgard

The sun does not rise here.
It travels.

A tide of golden light circles a ring-shaped world, and everything it touches, it defines. Night is not darkness. Night is erasure.

At seven cycles, every child kneels and receives a Mark — a purpose, a place, a name the world agrees to remember. Farmer. Sun Guard. Archivist. The Mark is the anchor. The Mark is the promise that you will not be forgotten.

Some children receive nothing at all.

They are called the Undefined. The System does not record them, so the maps show them as static — black dots beneath the rivers of gold. Cleaning errors. Noise.

And lately, the noise has been going quiet. Whole settlements of them. They do not die — a death leaves its own mark on the map. They do not move. They simply cease to be data.

Erik Swordstrong was Defined. A captain of the Sun Guard, an anchor, a man the Light swore it would never forget. He kept that faith through the first Rift, through the loss of his sight, through five hundred rotations of hunting things that should not exist.

Then the horizon split, and the old, forbidden word came back.
