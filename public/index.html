The world of Aethergard

The sun does not rise here.
It travels.

Light here does not merely illuminate. It carries Ith — the binding that tells matter to hold its shape. Night is not darkness. Night is the binding loosening, which is the real reason nobody travels once the light has passed.

At six, every child kneels in the High Sanctum and the Luminarchs burn a Mark into the back of the neck. It fixes their function, their station and the manner of their death. Sun Guard. Archivist. Founder. The Mark is the anchor — the promise that the world has agreed to remember you.

Some children receive nothing at all.

They are called the Undefined. The System does not record them, so the maps show them as static — black dots beneath the rivers of gold. Cleaning errors. Noise.

And lately, the noise has been going quiet. Whole settlements of them. They do not die — a death leaves its own mark on the map. They do not move. They simply cease to be data.

Erik was Defined. A captain of the Sun Guard — and the only man to walk out of the first Rift alive, after killing every one of his own squad with his own sword. The Kingdom made him a hero, then exiled him for protecting an Undefined girl. He tore a strip from his captain's cape, tied it over his eyes, and has hunted by sound and pressure for five cycles since.

He is the only man alive who can feel that the light is going wrong.
