A NOTE FROM THE CARTOGRAPHER

A world, faithfully traced.

This atlas combines the original chart published on abyrith.com with the Book One manuscript and its world bible. The chart is an illustration, not a measured survey.

On the chart
A marker follows a recognisable feature in the original artwork. It does not imply precise distance or scale.
Regional record
The text establishes a place, but the chart does not locate it. Selecting it highlights its broader region. No street-level position is invented.
Unplaced
The source does not fix a reliable position. These records remain in the catalogue without a pin.
Connections & journeys
Dotted lines are reading guides between chart anchors, not surveyed roads. Journey stops can share a regional anchor.

The manuscript takes precedence where labels differ: Oakhaven is treated as a city within Aurelion. “Wydin?” remains the chart’s uncertain name; it is not silently identified with Eldwyn or Wydienor.

Eastern border: the author confirms that the Thunder Plains separate Solkar from the Wild Kingdom. The atlas follows the order Solkar → Thunder Plains → Wild Kingdom, overriding the original chart’s printed label placement. The storm belt shows this relationship schematically.

Book One plot developments are optional. Unpublished revelations from the author-only series canon are excluded. The author specifies clockwise world rotation under a fixed 40° beam. Sunrise proceeds from Aurelion toward Solkar, Thunder Plains, the Wild Kingdom, Wydin, and the Sapphire Basin. This overrides the original chart’s counterclockwise annotation. Animation speed is illustrative, not an in-world timetable.
